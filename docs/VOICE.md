# Voice and Terminology Guide

How Global AI Governance writes on every public surface: repository descriptions, READMEs, the Pages site, `llms.txt`, and quick starts. Use it for every new line of copy.

## Core essence

These three lines anchor everything else:

- **No AI system moves faster than ownership, evidence, authority, and control.**
- **Evidence before claims.**
- **AI assists; humans retain authority.**

Every tool reports and a person decides. Every output carries `authority_effect: NONE`.

## Who we write for

Founders and teams building with AI agents, along with the operators, engineers, and reviewers who work with them. Lead with the benefit to them: **govern your agents so you can give them more autonomy, safely.**

## Voice

| Principle | Do | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Plain language first | "Check your AI systems and agents before they act." | "Policy-driven governance pipeline for inventory records." |
| Benefit before mechanism | Say what the reader gets, then how it works. | Opening with schemas, digests, or contract names. |
| Explain before jargon | "Model Context Protocol (MCP), the open standard for AI assistants to call tools", then "MCP" | An unexplained acronym on first use. |
| Calm and confident | "The toolkit finds missing owners, monitoring, shutdown paths, and evidence." | "Revolutionary", "world-class", "guaranteed", "effortless". |
| Precise and true | State what was tested and how. | Invented adopters, statistics, outcomes, or promises. |
| Short sentences | One idea per sentence. Use active voice. | Chains of clauses joined by semicolons. |
| Show real commands | Show the command the reader actually runs, in a copyable code block. | Naming a command just to name it. |

## Never write

- Hype, superlatives, or urgency ("best", "revolutionary", "act now").
- Invented adopters, users, partners, statistics, or outcomes.
- Community, contact, social, or pilot-program copy, and calls to join, follow, or book a call.
- Any wording that suggests a tool approves, certifies, or authorizes deployment.
- "Hosted endpoint" claims. Global AI Governance operates no hosted MCP endpoint.

## Claims Integrity labels

Mark every capability claim with one of these labels:

- **TESTED**: run by this project's own CI or test runs (not independent verification).
- **SIMULATED**: exercised with synthetic inputs or a simulation only.
- **UNTESTED**: written or configured but not run.
- **TARGET**: intended future state, not delivered.

## Spelling

Use American English. The project wordlist below is accepted by spell checkers. `.codespellrc` in this repository carries the `codespell` exceptions.

`authority_effect`, CISO, gaig, gaig-mcp, Glasswing, llms, MCPB, OT (operational technology), Pydantic, SARIF, sdist, Semgrep, SHS (UNESCO Social and Human Sciences), Streamable, Typer, uvx, venv.

Use the hyphenated form for compound prefixes in prose, for example "pre-implementation" and "non-execution". Keep exact tokens such as maturity labels and enum values as they are published.
