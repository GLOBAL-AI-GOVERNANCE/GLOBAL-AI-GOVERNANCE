# NIST AI RMF 1.0 - Bounded Portfolio Mapping

**Mapping date:** 2026-08-17

This is an informative engineering crosswalk to the currently published **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0, NIST AI 100-1)**. NIST states that AI RMF 1.0 is being revised; this mapping therefore records the published 1.0 baseline and should be re-evaluated when NIST publishes a revision.

NIST describes AI RMF 1.0 as voluntary, rights-preserving, non-sector-specific, and use-case agnostic. The AI RMF Core uses four functions: **Govern, Map, Measure, Manage**. The NIST Playbook is guidance for those outcomes, not a checklist that must be followed in its entirety.

## Portfolio crosswalk

| AI RMF 1.0 function | GLOBAL AI GOVERNANCE evidence examples | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Govern** | Foundations doctrine and registers; Toolkit inventory, policy-driven findings, ownership, human decision records; portfolio maturity and authority boundaries | Repository artifacts support governance practice; they do not establish organizational adoption or compliance. |
| **Map** | Toolkit system inventory and context; risk tiering; ACRF system-boundary and authorized-flow modeling; documented assumptions and dependencies | Mapping quality depends on the truth and completeness of supplied system context. |
| **Measure** | Toolkit validation reports and Decision Pack evidence; Agentic conformance artifacts; VVD evidence/closure tests; ACRF evidence and invariant verification | Technical checks establish only the tested artifact behavior and supplied evidence state. |
| **Manage** | Human decision records; Agentic expiry/revocation; VVD remediation and closure; ACRF contain/recover/reverify/reauthorize semantics; Peace fictional decision/AAR context | Management and operational decisions remain human-owned and environment-specific. |

## Related lifecycle

The portfolio's engineering lifecycle - Govern -> Authorize -> Design Enforcement -> Observe -> Verify -> Contain -> Recover -> Measure -> Learn - is not presented as a replacement for the AI RMF Core. It is an internal lifecycle that can provide evidence relevant to Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage.

## Non-claim

This crosswalk **does not establish NIST compliance, certification, conformity, legal sufficiency, production authorization, or organizational implementation**.
