# NYC Validation Case Study (Proposed Legislation)

**Status: Proposed. Not law. Source-locked 2026-10-14.**

Primary source: one-page prepared remarks submitted for the New York City Council Committee of the Whole hearing on artificial intelligence, 5 October 2026. Not a full transcript.

## Source claims (not established facts by this record)

The remarks assert serious present harms associated with general-purpose systems and related generative technologies, and argue that independent pre-deployment review is warranted. Specific quantitative or institutional allegations require independent evidence before treatment as established facts. The remarks reject imminent human extinction as the primary framing.

## Proposed controls

Official legislative records describe proposals that would condition marketing, sale, or deployment of covered artificial intelligence models in New York City on third-party validation and the presence of a human-operable shut-down capability. Assessment domains referenced in the records include task performance, calibration, behavior under distribution shift, data provenance, disparate impact, data handling, and safety. Evaluator interest disclosure, deployment-readiness findings, rulemaking for qualifications, and per-instance civil penalties are also described. Companion proposals address protected reporting of AI-related public-health or public-safety concerns and civilian enforcement incentives.

Status as of source lock: Introduction. Not enacted.
