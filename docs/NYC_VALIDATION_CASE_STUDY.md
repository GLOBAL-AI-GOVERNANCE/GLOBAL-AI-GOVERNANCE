# NYC Validation Case Study (Proposed Legislation)

**Status: Proposed. Not law. Source-locked 2026-10-14.**

Primary source: prepared remarks submitted for the NYC City Council Committee of the Whole hearing on artificial intelligence, 5 October 2026. One-page document, not a full transcript.

## Source claims (not established facts by this record)

- General-purpose agents with broad internet access have led to large numbers of hacking incidents.
- Chatbots have been implicated in severe human harms.
- Federal institutions have not enacted equivalent independent review requirements.
- Concerns have been raised regarding retaliation against individuals reporting serious issues.

The remarks reject imminent human extinction as the primary framing while arguing for serious present and emerging harms.

## Proposed controls (T2026-2602 and companions)

- Independent third-party validation before market access or deployment in NYC.
- Defined assessment domains (task performance including accuracy, precision and recall, calibration, behavior under distribution shift; determinism; latency and throughput; data provenance; disparate impact and bias; lawful and secure data handling; safety).
- Human-operable shut-down capability.
- Evaluator interest disclosure.
- Deployment-readiness determination.
- Rulemaking authority for validator qualifications and additional criteria.
- Per-instance civil penalties applicable to both the covered party and the validator.
- Companion measures addressing whistleblower protections and civilian enforcement incentives.

Bills placed on agenda 8 October 2026. Status as of source lock: Introduction. Not enacted.
