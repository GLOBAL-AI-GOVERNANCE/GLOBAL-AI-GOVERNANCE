# Source Policy

Public claims should be traceable to suitable evidence and phrased no more strongly than that evidence supports.

## Source discipline

- Prefer primary standards, official publications, original research, and authoritative project documentation for factual claims.
- Distinguish source assertions from portfolio interpretation or recommendation.
- Record versions, publication dates, or stable identifiers when freshness matters.
- Do not copy restricted third-party material into a repository merely because it is publicly viewable.
- Attribute reused material according to its license.
- Recheck time-sensitive claims before releases or major public updates.

Repository validation can establish internal consistency of included artifacts. It does not independently establish the truth of external claims.
